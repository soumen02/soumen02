# Soumen Mohanty

**Technical generalist. Curious about AI, products, startups, and what to build next.**

I love exploring what new technology makes possible, especially AI. The part that pulls me in is figuring out where it could be useful: finding a problem, shaping an idea, building something, and seeing how people use it.

I'm drawn to product and startups because the work crosses boundaries. I like wearing several hats: talking to users, thinking through the product, prototyping, connecting systems, and helping people adopt what gets built. The domain can change; that mix is what interests me.

### Things that keep me curious

- **AI as a building tool:** Exploring Claude and Codex workflows, prototyping ideas, and helping others use these tools. I keep responsibility for testing and decisions, with human review inside the AI workflows I design.
- **Product judgment:** Understanding the actual problem, deciding what is worth building, and making the result usable.
- **Startups and small teams:** The variety and ownership of moving between ideas, technology, and people.
